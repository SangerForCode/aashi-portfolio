Popular types of therapy

Some therapy modalities are better suited to treating specific mental health conditions than others. Learn more about common types of therapy and how to find a practitioner who specializes in each.

Cognitive behavioral therapy

Cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) is a structured, evidence-based therapy focused on identifying and changing negative thought patterns and behaviors. It can be highly effective for treating anxiety, depression, PTSD, and OCD by helping individuals develop healthier coping strategies. CBT is widely used for many clients, including those experiencing stress, phobias, and mood disorders.

Learn More

Dialectical behavior therapy

Dialectical behavior therapy (DBT) combines cognitive-behavioral techniques with mindfulness to help individuals manage emotions, improve relationships, and tolerate distress. It was originally developed for borderline personality disorder (BPD) but can also be used for other conditions, including self-harming behaviors, suicidal thoughts, and eating disorders. DBT is particularly beneficial for individuals struggling with emotional dysregulation and intense interpersonal conflicts.

Learn More

Exposure Therapy

Exposure therapy is a form of cognitive-behavioral treatment that helps individuals reduce anxiety and avoidance behaviors by gradually confronting feared situations, objects, or memories. It's highly effective for phobias, PTSD, OCD, and social anxiety disorder. Individuals learn to reduce distress and improve daily functioning by facing fears in a controlled setting.

Learn More

Acceptance and commitment therapy

Acceptance and commitment therapy (ACT) uses mindfulness and values-based action to help individuals accept difficult emotions and thoughts rather than fight them. It's particularly useful for anxiety, depression, chronic pain, and trauma-related disorders. ACT empowers individuals to live meaningful lives despite emotional challenges, making it useful for those struggling with distress tolerance and self-acceptance.

Learn More
